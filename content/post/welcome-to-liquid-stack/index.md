Liquid Stack is a Hugo starter that gives a new site a calm, polished foundation without tying it to a particular person, domain, or service.

## Start with your identity

Change the title, URL, sidebar copy, and social links in `hugo.yaml`. Then replace the abstract avatar and social-preview image in `static/img/liquid-stack/` with your own assets.

## Add your writing

Create a directory in `content/post/` for each article. An English post uses `index.md`; add `index.zh.md` alongside it for a Simplified Chinese translation. Keep a stable `slug` once an article is public.

## Keep integrations optional

Comments and analytics are disabled in the sample configuration. The optional CMS and page-view Worker include placeholder settings and should be configured with your own accounts only when needed.
