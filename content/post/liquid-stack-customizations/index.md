Liquid Stack keeps the writing workflow of Hugo and Stack while adding pages suited to a portfolio or personal site.

## Home page and sidebar

The home page brings recent posts, shortcuts, and widgets together. Sidebar items are controlled by page front matter; keep the original icons and order, then change only labels or links where needed.

## Projects and visual content

Launchpad entries live in `data/launchpad/` and can include an icon, preview, article URL, and repository URL. Photo-wall entries live in `data/photo-wall/`, with images under `static/img/gallery/`.

## Admin and dashboard

The project retains its admin entry, dashboard, and static CMS configuration. Replace example URLs and keys before connecting your own services, and never commit real credentials to a public repository.
